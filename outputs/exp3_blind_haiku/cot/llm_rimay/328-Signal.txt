TextSecure shall send a "dummy random message" to a random contact that uses TextSecure at a random time.
The sender shall enable the "dummy messaging feature" with a defined quantity parameter.
If battery level is below a threshold "eg. 30%", then TextSecure shall not send dummy messages.
If roaming is detected and not explicitly enabled by the user, then TextSecure shall not send dummy messages.
The recipient shall be able to opt-out of receiving dummy messages by notifying peers.
TextSecure shall send a "clear text dummy message" to a random contact that does not use TextSecure to promote its use.
TextSecure shall include a random peer's phone number in a dummy message without displaying it to the user.
