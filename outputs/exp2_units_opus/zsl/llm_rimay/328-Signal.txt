TextSecure must send "dummy random messages" to "random recipients among contacts that use TextSecure" at "random times".
When the recipient TextSecure receives a "dummy message", then the recipient TextSecure must discard the "dummy message" after decryption.
TextSecure must allow the sender to enable the "dummy messaging feature" as an "opt-in" option.
TextSecure must allow the sender to define the "desired quantity of dummy messages".
If the device is roaming and the sender has not enabled "dummy messaging while roaming", or the "battery level" is less than "the configured threshold", then TextSecure must not send "dummy messages".
