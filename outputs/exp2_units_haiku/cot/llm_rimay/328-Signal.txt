When the sender enables the "dummy message" feature, the System shall send dummy messages to random recipients selected from TextSecure contacts.
If the "dummy message" feature is enabled, the System shall inject dummy messages at randomized intervals to level-out legitimate message frequency and spoil traffic analysis.
If the sender defines a quota, the System shall enforce the sender-configured limits on dummy messages including maximum count per month, ratio to legitimate messages, and total message ceiling.
If roaming is disabled or battery level is below thirty percent, the System shall not send dummy messages.
When the sender enables the promotion mode, the System shall send plaintext dummy messages to non-TextSecure contacts with a maximum frequency of one message per recipient per month during daytime hours only.
