When the sender enables the dummy messaging feature and selects a TextSecure contact, then TextSecure shall send a "dummy random message" to the "randomly selected TextSecure contact".
When a "dummy message" is received and decrypted, then TextSecure shall automatically discard the "dummy message".
When the sender enables the cleartext dummy messaging extension and selects a non-TextSecure contact, then TextSecure shall send a "dummy message in clear text" to the "randomly selected non-TextSecure contact".
If roaming is enabled and battery level is above the "configured threshold" (such as 30%), then TextSecure shall send the "dummy message".
When the sender opts in to the dummy messaging feature, then TextSecure shall enable the sender to "configure dummy message parameters" including "maximum quantity per month", "ratio to legitimate messages", and "total message limit".
