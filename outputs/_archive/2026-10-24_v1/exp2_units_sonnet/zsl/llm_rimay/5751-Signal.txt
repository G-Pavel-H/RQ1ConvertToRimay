For the Signal-Android app, once the user has swiped away a notification, the app must not display that notification again. When the app receives another message from the same contact or number, it should show a new notification with the new message.
Rimay requirements:
If the user swipes away a notification of a received SMS, then the Signal-Android app must not display the swiped away notification.
When the Signal-Android app receives another message from the same contact or number, then the Signal-Android app must display a new notification with the new message.
