When the Sender enables the "dummy messaging feature", then TextSecure <MISSING_MODAL_VERB> send "dummy random messages" to "random recipients chosen among contacts that use TextSecure" at "random times".
When TextSecure decrypts a "dummy message" from the Sender, then TextSecure <MISSING_MODAL_VERB> discard the "dummy message" automatically.
TextSecure must activate the "dummy messaging feature" only upon "opt-in by the Sender" based on the "desired quantity of dummy messages defined by the Sender".
While the Device is roaming, TextSecure must disable the "dummy messaging feature" by default.
If Battery.Level is less than "30%", then TextSecure must not send "dummy messages".
