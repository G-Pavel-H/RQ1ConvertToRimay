For the "Image gallery", the System shall enable multiselection of images and media.
When the user multiselects images and media in the "Image gallery" mode, then the System shall delete the selected images and media.
When an image or media is opened, then the System shall provide the possibility to delete the opened image or media.
When the user initiates deletion of an image or media, then the System shall display a confirmation dialog to the user.
When deleting an image or media in gallery mode or opened view, then the System shall display a hint indicating the corresponding message text or deleted image and media context.
