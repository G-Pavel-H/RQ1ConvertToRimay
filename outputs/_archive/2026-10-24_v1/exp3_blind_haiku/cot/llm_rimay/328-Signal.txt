TextSecure shall send dummy random messages to randomly selected contacts that use TextSecure.
When a recipient receives a dummy message, the recipient shall recognize the message as such after decryption and automatically discard it.
When a sender enables the dummy messaging feature, TextSecure shall allow the sender to define desired quantity parameters.
If the device battery level is below 30%, then TextSecure shall not send dummy messages.
If roaming is enabled and the recipient does not use TextSecure, then TextSecure shall send a cleartext promotion message only during daytime hours.
A recipient shall be able to opt-out and notify peers that they do not want to receive dummy messages.
