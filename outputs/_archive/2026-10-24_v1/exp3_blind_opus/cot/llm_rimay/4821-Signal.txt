For each received message, when the user long-presses the "received message", then Signal <MISSING_MODAL_VERB> display the "acknowledge message" option to the user.
For each received message, when the user selects "acknowledge message", then Signal <MISSING_MODAL_VERB> replace the "received" indicator with an "acknowledgement indicator" for the message.
