The Signal app shall display a screen showing a "pie chart" that indicates how many of the user's contacts are on Signal.
The Signal app shall display a "pie chart" that indicates how many of the messages the user sends are encrypted.
When the user taps an element of the "pie chart", then the Signal app shall display a "list of people in the tapped category".
Where gamification is available, when most but not all of the user's contacts are using Signal, then the Signal app shall display a message such as "Almost there! __".
