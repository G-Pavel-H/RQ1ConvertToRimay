For all the contacts, the Signal app must display a "pie chart that indicates how many of the contacts are on Signal".
For all the messages, the Signal app must display a "pie chart that indicates how many of the sent messages are encrypted".
When the user taps an element of the pie chart, then the Signal app must display a "list of people in that category".
Where the app has gamification, when most but not all of the contacts are using Signal, then the Signal app must display a "message such as 'Almost there!'".
