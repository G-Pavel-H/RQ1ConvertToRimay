TextSecure shall send a "dummy random message" to "random recipients" at "random times".
When TextSecure decrypts a "dummy message", then TextSecure shall discard the "dummy message" automatically.
If the "battery level" is less than "30%", then TextSecure shall not send a "dummy message".
TextSecure shall allow the sender to define the "desired quantity of dummy messages".
While the device is roaming, TextSecure shall disable the "dummy messaging feature" by default.
