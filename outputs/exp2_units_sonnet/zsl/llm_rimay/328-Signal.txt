TextSecure shall send "dummy random messages" to "random recipients" at "random times".
TextSecure shall choose the recipients of the "dummy messages" randomly between "contacts that knowingly use TextSecure".
When TextSecure decrypts a "dummy message", then TextSecure shall discard the "dummy message" automatically.
TextSecure shall allow the sender to enable the "dummy messaging feature" by "opt-in" and to define the "desired quantity of dummy messages".
If the battery level is less than "30%", then TextSecure shall not send "dummy messages".
