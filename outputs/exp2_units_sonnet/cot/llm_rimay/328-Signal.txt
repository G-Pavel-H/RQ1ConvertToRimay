TextSecure shall send "dummy random messages" to "randomly chosen contacts that knowingly use TextSecure" at "random times".
When TextSecure decrypts a "dummy message", then TextSecure shall automatically discard the "dummy message".
TextSecure shall allow the sender to enable the "dummy messaging feature" by "opt-in" and to define the "desired quantity of dummy messages".
If the Battery.Level is less than "30%", then TextSecure shall not send "dummy messages".
While the device is roaming, TextSecure shall not send "dummy messages" by default.
