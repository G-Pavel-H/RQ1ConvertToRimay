TextSecure shall send "dummy random messages" to random recipients at random times.
TextSecure shall choose the recipients of "dummy messages" randomly among "contacts that knowingly use TextSecure".
When TextSecure decrypts a "dummy message", then TextSecure shall discard the "dummy message" automatically.
TextSecure shall provide an "opt-in setting" for the "dummy messaging feature" to the sender.
TextSecure shall allow the sender to define the "desired quantity of dummy messages".
While the device is roaming, TextSecure shall not send "dummy messages".
TextSecure shall allow the sender to enable the sending of "dummy messages" while roaming.
If Device.Battery_Level is less than "30%", then TextSecure shall not send "dummy messages".
TextSecure shall allow the recipient to inform peers that the recipient does not want to receive "dummy messages" (opt-out).
TextSecure shall send a "promotion message in clear text" to random "contacts that do not use TextSecure yet".
TextSecure shall allow the sender to define a "custom explanation" for the "promotion message".
TextSecure shall not send more than "1 promotion message per month" to a single recipient.
TextSecure shall send "promotion messages" only during "daytime".
TextSecure shall include the "phone number of a random participating peer" in each "dummy message".
TextSecure shall not show the "phone numbers contained in dummy messages" to the user.
TextSecure shall send "dummy messages" to the "phone numbers contained in dummy messages".
TextSecure shall spread "opt-outs" through "dummy messages".
TextSecure shall define a "TTL" for each "circulated phone number".
