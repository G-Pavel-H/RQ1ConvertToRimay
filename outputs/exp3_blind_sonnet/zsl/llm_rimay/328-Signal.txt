The TextSecure shall send "dummy random messages" to random recipients at random times.
The TextSecure shall choose the recipients of the "dummy messages" randomly from the contacts that use TextSecure.
The TextSecure shall adjust the "randomness" of the "dummy messages" to level out the legitimate messages.
When the TextSecure receives a "dummy message", then the TextSecure shall automatically discard the "dummy message" after decryption.
The TextSecure shall allow the sender to enable the "dummy messaging feature" as an "opt-in".
The TextSecure shall allow the sender to define the "desired quantity of dummy messages".
While the device is roaming, the TextSecure shall not send the "dummy messages" by default.
While the device is roaming, the TextSecure shall allow the sender to enable the sending of "dummy messages".
If the "battery level" is less than "30%", then the TextSecure shall not send the "dummy messages".
The TextSecure shall allow the recipient to notify peers of the recipient's "opt-out" from receiving "dummy messages".
The TextSecure shall send a "default message" or a "custom explanation by the sender" in clear text to randomly chosen contacts that do not use TextSecure.
The TextSecure shall send at most "1 message" per month to a single contact that does not use TextSecure.
The TextSecure shall send the "messages to contacts that do not use TextSecure" only during "daytime".
The TextSecure shall include the "phone numbers of other peers participating in dummy messaging" in the "dummy messages".
The TextSecure shall not show the "phone numbers of other peers contained in dummy messages" to the user.
The TextSecure shall use the "phone numbers of other peers contained in dummy messages" as recipients of "dummy messages".
The TextSecure shall spread the "opt-outs" through the "dummy messages" by "direct reply dummy message" or by defining a "TTL" for each "phone number".
